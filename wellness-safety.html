ALTERSTATE — Wellness & Safety

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Not a medical device or treatment. Alterstate is a wellness tool for relaxation, focus, and reflection. It is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or psychiatric care, and it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

How the Sessions Work

Alterstate's binaural tones, isochronic pulses, and generative audio are designed to support relaxation, focus, and sleep. Its hypnotic inductions use spoken guidance and suggestion for relaxation and self-reflection — they are self-guided wellness content, not clinical hypnotherapy administered by a licensed practitioner.

Before You Begin

  • Use headphones in a private, comfortable place where you can safely close your eyes and relax, especially for hypnotic inductions.
  • Never listen while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that requires your full alertness. These sessions are designed to relax you and can affect reaction time.
  • Keep the volume at a moderate, comfortable level. Extended listening at high volume can affect your hearing over time, regardless of the content.
  • Alterstate is intended for users aged 13 and older.

When to Be Extra Careful

Talk to a doctor before using binaural tones, isochronic pulses, or hypnotic content if you:

  • Have epilepsy or a seizure disorder
  • Have a diagnosed mental health condition such as psychosis, bipolar disorder, or dissociative disorder
  • Are pregnant
  • Use a pacemaker or other implanted medical device
  • Are currently under psychiatric care or taking medication that affects your mood, alertness, or perception

During and After a Session

If you feel dizzy, disoriented, unusually anxious, or otherwise unwell during a session, stop and remove your headphones. Give yourself a moment before returning to normal activity, especially before driving. If discomfort continues, speak with a healthcare provider.

If You're in Crisis

Alterstate is not equipped to respond to emergencies and does not monitor your sessions in real time. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services, or a crisis line such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US), Samaritans on 116 123 (UK & Ireland), or the international directory at findahelpline.com.

Health Data

If you connect Apple Health, Alterstate uses signals like heart rate and HRV only to personalize recommendations — it does not interpret this data medically or flag health concerns. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's read and written.
